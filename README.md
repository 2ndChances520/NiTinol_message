## 📌 The Concept

**Nitinol** (Nickel Titanium) is a unique shape-memory alloy. No matter how severely it is bent, twisted, or distorted by external force, applying the right thermal energy causes it to instantly return to its original, perfectly engineered form.

This project is a spiritual and structural analogy:

> **Humanity gets bent out of shape by life, circumstances, and sin—but we are bound by a higher design.** 
> When subjected to the refined truth of God's law and grace, we don't break; we return to the exact form we were created to hold.

## ⚙️ Core Principles

| State | Physical Analogy (Nitinol) | Spiritual Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Original State** | Annealed to a master shape | Created in the image and intentional design of God. |
| **Deformation** | Twisted by stress & outer force | Bent by world pressure, temptation, and departure from truth. |
| **The Catalyst** | Refiner’s Fire / Heat | Conviction, Truth, and Divine alignment. |
| **Restoration** | Returns to original shape | Straightened out—restored to original purpose. |
